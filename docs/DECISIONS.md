## CONFIRMED — approved concept, NOT IMPLEMENTED
World Championships (MS) V1. Approved by owner 2026-11-14; implementation postponed until leagues/sub-leagues, scoring and scheduled jobs exist. Full text: `GAME_DESIGN.md` section 10.
- C-015 — MS happen every season in week 6, one per league (Rookie, Amateur, Continental, Pro, Elite). One single-day race (not a stage Tour), mixed terrain (flat, hills, climbs); the profile may change by season. MS do not count toward the league Tour limit. Participation is voluntary and by invitation only. Nationality does not affect qualification. Olympics, continental championships and other special races are out of scope.
- C-016 — Qualification: at the end of week 4 an immutable snapshot of the league standings per sub-league (season, league, sub-league, rider, manager, rank, points). Week-5 results never change nomination. Per sub-league: top 35% of eligible active riders are direct entrants, next 15% are reserves; both rounded up; reserves never exceed the remaining eligible riders (10→4+2, 20→7+3, 30→11+5, 40→14+6, 50→18+8). The snapshot must reflect results valid at the end of week 4 even if processed late.
- C-017 — Invitations: sent Monday of week 5 to direct entrants and reserves; each may accept or decline. Direct: accept = guaranteed spot, decline frees it. Reserve: accept = agrees to start if a spot frees up (not a guarantee); once promoted no re-confirmation. A freed spot goes to the next reserve by rank who already accepted; a higher-ranked undecided reserve must not be overtaken before the deadline (spot stays reserved).
- C-018 — Deadline: Friday of week 5, proposed end of Friday UTC game time (exact boundary to be defined at implementation). After it: unconfirmed invitations expire, no confirmation = not participating, freed spots filled by confirmed reserves in rank order (declined/unconfirmed skipped); if too few confirmed reserves the start list stays smaller; nobody starts without own consent; declining/expiry has no penalty (points, money or otherwise).
- C-019 — Championships: each league has its own MS; riders of different sub-leagues of one league race together. Above a configurable capacity (preliminary 100 per championship) the field is split into several championships (MS Rookie A, B, C...), evenly by count and by qualification rank so top riders are not concentrated (80→1; 120→2×60; 250→3×83–84; 500→5×100). Each championship has its own winner and world-champion title; no joint final yet. Elite has one sub-league, hence one championship. Race-engine field-size limits are NOT yet verified.
- C-020 — MS dashboard states: before qualification (MS, week 6, qualification at end of week 4); direct invitation (congratulations, rank, deadline, Accept/Decline); reserve invitation (reserve rank, conditions, Accept/Decline, then "waiting for a free spot"); after promotion (spot gained, "participation confirmed"); after deadline (assigned championship and date, or "not participating"). MS are shown in the calendar to players without an invitation too.
- C-021 — MS scope excluded for now: race simulation, results and titles, rainbow jerseys, rewards/bonuses/points, new training/energy/recovery mechanics.

## CONFIRMED — leagues and sub-leagues V1 (2026-11-14, implemented; needs DB migration deployed)
- C-022 — Five leagues: Rookie, Amateur, Continental, Pro, Elite. Each sub-league has at most 50 players. Sub-leagues are labelled `<League> 1.<n>` (Rookie 1.1, Rookie 1.2, ...) and are equal in strength; the number says nothing about rank. Elite has at most one sub-league, Elite 1.1 (50 players). Other sub-leagues are created on demand; no empty sub-leagues are created in advance.
- C-023 — A new player always starts in Rookie, placed in the lowest-numbered Rookie sub-league with a free spot; if all are full a new one is created. The player joins the CURRENT global season and week (no personal "Season 1"). A Tour that already started cannot be joined late; the player prepares for the next available Tour.
- C-024 — Test accounts may be assigned to sub-leagues; game progress is reset before the official launch.
- C-026 — Tour selection: a Tour whose week has started (or passed) cannot be joined; already-selected Tours stay selected. Enforced in `getSelectionState` and `registerForTour`. Source: owner 2026-11-14.
- C-027 — Season-end sub-league balancing (approved CONCEPT, NOT IMPLEMENTED; no algorithm yet). At the end of a season sub-leagues of a league are balanced by the number of ACTIVE vs INACTIVE players. Example: Rookie 1.1 has 20 active + 30 inactive, Rookie 1.2 has 40 active + 10 inactive → after balancing both have 30 active + 20 inactive. Rules: capacity stays 50; accounts and memberships are never deleted merely for inactivity; a player can return to the game at any time; existing players are NOT moved between sub-leagues during a season; balancing runs after the season ends, AFTER promotions and relegations; goal = as even a number of active players as possible with the fewest moves; applies to Rookie, Amateur, Continental and Pro; Elite has a single sub-league and is never balanced. Source: owner 2026-11-14.
- C-028 — Security: league, sub-league and joined season are server-controlled. `league_memberships` is the authoritative placement; `profiles.league` is a copy synced by a trusted trigger. Players may update only `display_name` and `preferred_language` on `profiles`; the placement function takes no client input and derives the season from server time in SQL. Implemented in migrations 01–03; resolves O-007 once deployed. Source: owner 2026-11-14.
- C-025 — Not decided / out of scope for league V1: promotion and relegation (and counts), inactivity and removal rules, scoring and tie-breaks, MS, cron/automatic race processing, race engine.

## CONFIRMED — rider score and ability radar (2026-11-14, implemented)
- C-029 — A rider has ONE player-facing rating: "Celkové hodnotenie" (OVR), see C-032. The separate "Výkon" score was removed from the Rider page and the Market table. The earlier 0–100 "Celkové skóre" (`riderOverall`, weights 65/15/15/5) is LEGACY: no longer shown, kept unchanged for internal logic/tests. Source: owner 2026-11-14.
- C-032 — Celkové hodnotenie (OVR) = Výkon × 0.50 + Taktika × 0.25 + Technika × 0.20 + Skúsenosti × 0.05, on the attribute scale (≈100–200), computed from UNROUNDED group means, rounded only at the end. Single shared function `overallRating()` (lib/rider/score.ts) used by the rider profile header and the Market table. Rozvoj, Potenciál, Trénovateľnosť, Profesionalita, Regenerácia and condition never enter it. Source: owner 2026-11-15 ("approved calculation").
- C-033 — Rider profile header: portrait slot (3:4, neutral; placeholder silhouette when no image) + name/nationality + age · style + real league label + Celkové hodnotenie; condition meters on the right. Detail group cards show a short description under the heading and the group average (Výkon · 128). "Hlavné oblasti" card removed; card "Silné stránky jazdca" is in place (content = next task; currently the existing top-5 attribute list). No portraits are generated or assigned yet. Source: owner 2026-11-15.
- C-034 — Card "Silné stránky jazdca" (informational only, no bonuses): the 3 highest VISIBLE skills from Výkon/Taktika/Technika (real values; ties broken by fixed group order Výkon→Taktika→Technika). Excluded: Skúsenosti, group averages, overall rating, hidden development attributes, condition, equipment. Below it a small "Pretekársky profil" = the existing rider style + short description; NO suitability calculation exists in the project, so none was invented (no new specialisations/talents). Source: owner 2026-11-15.
- C-035 — Rider → Prehľad layout (desktop): left = Radar schopností; right column = Silné stránky jazdca, then Rozvoj; below = Výkon/Taktika/Technika/Skúsenosti cards. Mobile order: radar, strengths, Rozvoj, detailed attributes. Rozvoj shows only the existing star/segment levels (no numeric rating on the radar) plus a note that more precise scouting of development attributes comes later. Radar has a subtle teal gradient + low-contrast topographic contour background behind the plot only (design only, no mechanics). Source: owner 2026-11-15.
- C-030 — Rider → Prehľad radar has five axes: Výkon, Taktika, Technika, Skúsenosti, Rozvoj. Group value = mean of its real attributes (same helpers as the overall score). One group can be expanded at a time (Výkon, Taktika, Technika, Rozvoj); Skúsenosti is a single point. Expanding only adds points and never changes the group averages or the polygon. Rozvoj is NOT part of the overall score. Source: owner 2026-11-14.
- C-031 — Rozvoj in the radar shows ONLY manager-visible scouting estimates (ranges), never true hidden values. No numeric scouting ranges exist yet (scouting gives only a star bucket for potential), so Rozvoj is shown as "Neznámy" (not 0, not plotted). A joint interval is computed (mean of lower bounds .. mean of upper bounds, shared 55–95 scale) only when ALL four attributes have an estimate. The data source is `lib/rider/developmentEstimates.ts` (server-only, currently all null); no new scouting rules were invented. Source: owner 2026-11-14.
